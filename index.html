About

I practice dentistry the way I'd want it for myself — unhurried, precise, and centered on the person in the chair. My clinical work covers scaling and root planing, root canal therapy, restorations, and routine preventive care.

Good dentistry sits at the intersection of science and trust. I take the time to explain every step, listen to what's actually worrying you, and tailor treatment to fit — because clarity and comfort matter as much as the work itself.
