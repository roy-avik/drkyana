About

About Me

As a fresh dental graduate, I bring up-to-date training, modern technique, and a calm chairside manner to every patient I see. My clinical experience covers scaling and root planing, root canal therapy (RCT), restorations, and routine preventive care.

I believe great dentistry is equal parts science and trust. I take time to explain procedures, listen to concerns, and tailor treatment to each patient — because comfort and clarity matter as much as the work itself.
